Privacy notice · release candidate

Report data stays with the Confluence macro.

This notice describes the release candidate reviewed on 1 October 2026, submitted to Atlassian Marketplace as version 2.3.0.

Current status

Rollup is submitted for Atlassian Marketplace review and is not yet approved or available for public purchase.

Operator and contact

Rollup is operated by Xavier Velasco Llauradó, an individual based in Spain. For privacy, support, or security questions, contact rollup@xvelasco.com.

What the app processes

Authors may enter or import report names, column names, cell values, formulas, grouping choices, and summary choices. Rollup processes that data to render the macro editor and reader.

Where app data goes

The release candidate saves the report document in the Confluence macro configuration through Atlassian Forge. Calculations run in the signed-in user’s browser. The audited app has no vendor-operated application server, database, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or external network egress.

Because the report is part of Confluence content, access, page history, retention, exports, backups, and deletion are governed by the customer’s Atlassian site configuration and Atlassian’s services. Space and page administrators should apply suitable Confluence permissions to sensitive reports.

What the app does not intentionally collect

The release candidate does not intentionally send report contents, formula values, user identifiers, or usage events to the developer. It does not set its own analytics cookies or embed third-party tracking resources.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send, such as your email address, app version, error text, and troubleshooting context. Do not send confidential report data, credentials, authentication tokens, or real CSV files. Support correspondence is used to investigate and respond to the request.

Operator responsibilities and lawful bases

Xavier Velasco Llauradó is the controller for support and security correspondence and customer administration records received through Marketplace. Contact details and correspondence are used to answer requests and maintain secure software, based on legitimate interests, or to take steps toward or perform a contract where applicable. Commercial records are also retained where required by tax and accounting law. Providing support contact information is voluntary, but a reply requires a contact route.

The current app supplies software that runs in the customer’s browser and stores report configuration in the customer’s Confluence page. The operator does not receive or host customer report data and does not act as a processor for those reports under this operating model. Customers remain responsible for their report content and Atlassian service arrangements. Support is limited to synthetic reproductions; the operator does not request production report access. No separate customer DPA is offered for this model. This assessment must be revisited before adding vendor processing, telemetry, remote support access, or other data services.

Mail providers and retention

Cloudflare Email Routing forwards the support address to Gmail; these providers process mail routing and message data under their own service and data-protection terms. Mail services may process data internationally. The operator keeps correspondence only as needed to resolve requests, maintain a relevant security record, or meet legal obligations, and then deletes or anonymizes it. Customers should not send personal report rows or sensitive information.

Your privacy rights

Contact rollup@xvelasco.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to the operator’s processing where those rights apply. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your competent supervisory authority. For data saved in a Confluence report, contact your site administrator to exercise rights through the site’s content and retention controls.

This documentation website

These static pages contain no developer-added analytics, tracking scripts, remote fonts, or third-party media. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process technical access and security logs, including visitor IP addresses, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Those website-host records are separate from report data handled by the Confluence app.

Deletion and uninstall

The app does not maintain a separate vendor database to delete. Removing a macro or Confluence page removes it according to the site’s Atlassian content lifecycle. In a development uninstall test on 19 September 2026, existing reports stopped rendering and Confluence retained a blank macro area; reinstalling the same app restored rendering without changing the page body or version. This is scoped test evidence, not a guarantee about every Atlassian retention or backup system.

Security and questions

Report a suspected vulnerability privately to rollup@xvelasco.com. Include a concise reproduction and impact, but no secrets or real customer data. See the support page for details.
